# Retention experiment sizing

Generated by `python scripts/experiment_sizing.py --effect 0.05`. Inputs for the sample-size calculation in `docs/experiment_plan.md`; no experiment outcomes are measured or simulated here.

## Inputs

| Input | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligible customers | 523 | test split customers at or above the profit threshold 0.34, the app default |
| Eligible share | 37.1% | same, as a share of the 1,409 test customers |
| Control retention rate | 46.3% | observed share of eligible test customers with Churn = No |
| Significance level, power | 0.05, 0.8 | two-sided test, 50/50 split |

The IBM dataset documentation describes Churn as customers who left within the last month, so it is only a proxy for the planned 90-day retention metric.

## Sample size

Detecting +5.0 pp over a 46.3% baseline needs **1,568 customers per arm** (3,136 in total).

| Minimum detectable effect | Customers per arm | Total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| +2.0 pp | 9,781 | 19,562 |
| +3.0 pp | 4,351 | 8,702 |
| +5.0 pp | 1,568 | 3,136 |
| +10.0 pp | 391 | 782 |

The test split has 523 eligible customers, so it is too small for this effect. Applying the same eligible share to all 7,043 customers gives about 2,614 eligible customers, which is still not enough for a +5.0 pp MDE.
